# Summary of changes (submitted version → revised version 2)

## 1. Correction of dataset identification (not requested by the reviewers; disclosed in the response letter and cover letter)
- Every sample re-verified against GEO/SRA metadata and by a sequence-based strain-identity check (Methods, *Sample identity verification*; Supplementary Table S1; Supplementary Fig. S1).
- Pure-strain comparison re-derived from correctly identified animals: 36 FVB/NJ + 34 C57BL/6J in four tissues from two laboratories (adipose, hypothalamus, liver: GSE123875/GSE123893/GSE123894; skin: GSE86315); diet/treatment covariates; control-condition-only sensitivity analysis.
- Datasets of the submitted version that contained no FVB-versus-B6 contrast are no longer used as such: GSE135230 excluded (single mixed background); GSE175625 (B6-congenic PyVT tumours) used as a negative control.
- Catalog criterion, filters and statistics unchanged and fixed before the analysis; catalog now 138 genes (0.50% of 27,732 tested; 24 of the original 32 retained). Worked examples chosen by rule: *Ndufs5* (SNP-induced multi-mapping) and *Rps2* (direct SNP misalignment).
